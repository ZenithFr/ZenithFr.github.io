06. DESIGN.MD OPEN STANDARD

Inspired by the open standard for AI-generated UI, DESIGN.md acts as the unified context bridge between human designers and AI coding agents. It merges machine-readable YAML design tokens with human-readable markdown prose.

Why DESIGN.md?

  • Unified Context: Keeps design variables and rules in one single truth file.
  • Semantic Roles: Teaches AI to treat tokens as semantic constraints, not just variables.
  • Validation: Ensures AI updates maintain brand consistency before output.
